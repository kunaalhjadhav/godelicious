# Legal document drafts

These are **plain-language starting drafts** for the three legal pages Razorpay (and good
practice generally) expects before going live: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and a
Refund/Cancellation Policy.

**These are not legal advice.** Have them reviewed by a qualified professional for your
jurisdiction before relying on them.

The live, editable versions are the actual website pages — these ARE the source, there's no
separate copy to keep in sync:
- `customer-web/app/privacy/page.js`
- `customer-web/app/terms/page.js`
- `customer-web/app/refund-policy/page.js`

The content in each is plain English wrapped in minimal HTML tags — readable directly, or copy
the text out into a plain document if you'd rather send a lawyer something that isn't code.

After a lawyer signs off (or you finalize your own decisions), copy the final wording back into
the three page files above so the live site matches.
